Architectural distillation of Google Garf (v0.2.0-core, 588 commits, commit dad42091), an enterprise framework unifying reporting APIs (Google Ads, Analytics, YouTube, REST) behind declarative SQL queries. Details query compilation, dynamic date macro resolution (:YYYYMMDD-N), in-memory GarfReport matrices, heterogeneous analytical writers (BigQuery, DuckDB, SQLite), deterministic zero-network simulation, and two-stage DAG workflow execution.
