## Executive Summary
Distilling unstructured, long-form multimodal artifacts (video transcripts, conference lectures, podcasts) into actionable AI capabilities requires distinct cognitive pathways. Conflating conceptual understanding with executable instructions produces bloated skills and speculative knowledge. The `MediaMindForge` architecture bifurcates analysis into two concurrent lenses:
1. **The Epistemic Introspection Lens (Mind-Reader)**: Analyzes ontological assumptions, mental models, decision heuristics, and delta learnings into ground-truth Knowledge Items (KIs) tied to exact video timestamps.
2. **The Procedural Skill Synthesis Lens (Book-to-Skill Forge)**: Translates domain methodologies into operational agent skills (`SKILL.md` + `CARD.md`) featuring Shu-Ha-Ri progression, diagnostic scorecards, and anti-pattern guardrails.

## Architectural Invariants & Rules
1. **Separation of Concerns**: Never interleave declarative domain theories into executable skill steps; commit theories to the Knowledge Vault as KIs and reference them from skills.
2. **Isnad Timestamp Grounding**: Spoken claims distilled from video/audio transcripts must record second-level timestamp offsets (`timestamp_s`) and speaker attribution.
3. **Codification**: Formally codified as `AGENTS.md` Rule 41.
