# Design Memo Q2 — Abstention & Human Escalation Policy for `uncertainty_guard`

**Design Question:** *"How should `uncertainty_guard` decide to abstain or escalate to a human?"*

## Recommendation

Replace single-model softmax thresholds and unweighted majority voting in `uncertainty_guard` with a **3-stage shift-resilient abstention and human-escalation policy**:

1. **Ensemble Marginalization & Confidence-Weighted Aggregation:** Under distribution shift, post-hoc temperature scaling on a single checkpoint is systematically overconfident compared to ensembles (`ki_google_research_uq_benchmark_2019_calibration_shift`, Verified). Aggregate multiple traces/checkpoints via temperature-scaled `SOFTMAX` or `LINEAR` `majority_with_conf()`, keeping zero-confidence filtering, `NaN`/empty guards, and `TIE` fallback (`ki_google_research_cisc_confidence_voting`, Verified).
2. **Dual-Threshold Confidence Band for Abstention vs. Human Escalation:** Use an ASPEST-style three-zone gate parameterized by `[lower_threshold, upper_threshold)` on max ensemble probability / normalized answer confidence (`ki_google_research_aspest_active_selective_prediction`, Verified):
   - **Below `lower_threshold`:** **Abstain** safely (reject output / invoke fallback).
   - **Inside `[lower_threshold, upper_threshold)` (narrow margin band):** **Escalate to Human / Oracle** under a bounded query budget (`AverageMarginSampling`-style marginal prioritization).
   - **At or above `upper_threshold`:** **Accept** autonomously.
3. **Shift-Aware Threshold Calibration Metric Suite:** Calibrate thresholds offline with confidence-coverage selectivity curves (`compute_accuracies_at_confidences()`) and `brier_decomposition()` (uncertainty/resolution/reliability) (`ki_google_research_uq_benchmark_2019_calibration_shift`, Verified), alongside Within-Question Discrimination (WQD) and trace-efficiency metrics (`calculate_metrics_at_position()`, `>40%` path reduction vs standard self-consistency, `ki_google_research_cisc_confidence_voting`, Verified).

## Conflicts & Gaps

1. **[CONFLICT] Global Calibration (`ECE` + Temperature Scaling) vs. Relative Within-Question Discrimination (`WQD`):** `ki_google_research_cisc_confidence_voting` prioritizes WQD over global ECE and uses temperature-scaled `SOFTMAX` weights across candidate reasoning paths, whereas `ki_google_research_uq_benchmark_2019_calibration_shift` shows post-hoc temperature scaling still breaks under covariate/domain shift for *absolute* abstention probabilities. Thus, WQD suffices to pick the best candidate trace, while human-escalation cutoffs require ensemble/margin calibration.
2. **[CONFLICT] Online Checkpoint Self-Training vs. Stateless Guard Determinism:** `ki_google_research_aspest_active_selective_prediction` couples abstention with iterative checkpoint KL self-training inside `[lower_threshold, upper_threshold)`, which conflicts with deterministic stateless verification in `uncertainty_guard`. Resolution: use ASPEST's confidence band and margin escalation online, and batch human oracle feedback into offline calibration sweeps (`compute_accuracies_at_confidences`) rather than online weight mutation.
3. **[GAP] Missing Harness-Specific Numeric Thresholds & Graph Link Sparsity:** Retrieved evidence gives algorithms (`SOFTMAX`/`LINEAR`, `compute_accuracies_at_confidences()`, confidence bands) but supplies no Brain Harness-calibrated numeric defaults for `lower_threshold`, `upper_threshold`, or `TemperatureScaling`. Additionally, `ki_google_research_cisc_confidence_voting` lacked a `references_code` link to `harness_services_uncertainty_guard`, and `ki_njoku_uncertainty_aware_systems` was excluded by graph retrieval (`isnad_verified=False`, 0 `references_code` edges).
